# AGILE TEAM CHARTER

## 1. Purpose & Core Values

* **Purpose:** To develop professional technical and testing skills, enhancing intern employability through immersive, project-based learning.
* **Core Values:** Continuous improvement, integrity, mutual trust, and high-quality software delivery aligned with DevOps principles.

## 2. Communication Protocols

* **Primary Channels:** 
  * **Microsoft Teams:** Main communication platform and project meetings.
  * **Telegram:** Quick team syncs.
  * **Kintai:** Daily time tracking and timesheet management.
  * **Outlook:** Internal scheduling and communication.
* **Daily Sync Schedule:** 
  * **Dev Scrum:** 09:00 - 09:15 daily
  * **Daily Scrum:** 09:15 - 09:30 daily
* **Response Times:** Facilitator responses to queries range from immediate to one hour.

## 3. Team Roles

* **Interns:** Focused on skill acquisition, task execution, continuous learning, and demonstrating proficiency in industry-standard tooling.
* **Facilitator:** Serves as a mentor, guide, and evaluator of intern performance and technical understanding.

## 4. Definition of Done (DoD)

A task is considered "Done" when:
1. The code or test script executes and runs successfully.
2. The intern successfully completes a one-on-one demo with the facilitator, verifying their understanding of the sprint's technical objectives.
