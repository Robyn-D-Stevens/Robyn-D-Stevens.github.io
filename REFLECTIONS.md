# REFLECTIONS

## Agile Team Charter Reflection

During onboarding, our team norms and communication tools were established, specifically the use of Telegram. My role as an intern was clearly emphasized from the start, allowing me to understand my position and responsibilities within the broader project structure. Afterwards, we reviewed company policies regarding communication topics such as data privacy, information sharing and the hierarchy of who is responsible for what at the company.


## User Stories and Tasks Reflection

Project requirements were translated into specific tasks through detailed Word documents provided by our facilitator. These documents outlined the prerequisites and resources, while hands-on coding practice and documentation research fostered problem-solving skills through trial and error.


## Product Backlog Reflection

My project goals were prioritized by first addressing university commitments. Then during work hours, the facilitator’s guidance through structured Word docs ensured that tasks were managed and executed effectively.


## Sprint Backlog Reflection

Following a continuous waterfall method, I managed weekly progress by referencing monthly report registers and setting bookmarks for personal daily checkpoints to ensure steady advancement alongside time management.


## Agile Logbook Reflection

Daily stand-ups are conducted through the Daily Scrum Teams channel, where I provided status updates and highlighted any upskilling. Issues and blockers were resolved through direct communication with the facilitator, often handled with quick messages or separate calls outside of scrum seeing as the time any issues/blockers occur is usually during the rest of the workday and do not flow into the next due to communication with the facilitator.
